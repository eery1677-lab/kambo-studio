Kambo Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Kambo Studio ("we", "the app") is a private content tool operated by Kambo AI Studio for publishing our own videos and posts to our own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, Threads, YouTube). It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information from other people.

1. What information we access

When the account owner connects one of our social media accounts to Kambo Studio through the platform's official login, the platform gives the app:

  • basic profile information of the connected account (display name, profile picture, account ID), and
  • an access token that lets the app publish posts or videos to that account and read their results.

We do not access followers' data, private messages, or any other user's information.

2. How we use it

  • To show which account is connected before posting.
  • To publish a post or video only after the account owner reviews and approves it.
  • To read the posting result and basic performance numbers of our own posts so the owner can review them.

3. Where it is stored

Access tokens and posting records are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and are not used for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account or they expire. Posting records are kept so the owner can review past posts and can be deleted at any time.

5. Your choices

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions lets you disconnect Kambo Studio at any time.
  • Instagram and Threads: you can remove the app in the account's Apps and websites / Website permissions settings.
  • To ask for deletion of any stored data, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
