Kambo Studio — Terms of Service

Last updated: October 27, 2026

These terms apply to Kambo Studio, a private content tool operated by Kambo AI Studio.

1. What the app does

Kambo Studio helps its owner prepare short videos and posts and publish them to the owner's own social media accounts (such as TikTok, Instagram and Threads) after the owner reviews and approves each one. Nothing is posted automatically without that approval.

2. Who may use it

The app is used only by Kambo AI Studio and the people it authorizes to manage its own accounts. It is not a public service and has no sign-up for other users.

3. Content

  • Only content that Kambo AI Studio owns or has the right to use is posted.
  • Posts must follow the rules of each platform, including the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines and Meta's terms for Instagram and Threads.
  • Where content was made with AI tools, it is labeled as required by each platform.

4. Connected accounts

The owner connects each account through the platform's official login and can disconnect it at any time in that platform's settings. See our Privacy Policy for how connection data is handled.

5. No warranty

The app is provided as is, for internal use. We do our best to keep it working, but we do not guarantee that it is free of errors or always available.
